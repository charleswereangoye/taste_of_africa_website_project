Smoky Jollof Rice

A party-sized pot with deep tomato flavor and the unmistakable char of a well-loved kitchen. Popular across West Africa, especially in Ghana, Nigeria, and Senegal.

Ingredients

  • Long-grain rice
  • Plum tomatoes
  • Red bell pepper
  • Onion
  • Garlic & ginger
  • Thyme
  • Stock
  • Scotch bonnet

Cooking Instructions

01 · Build the base

Blend plum tomatoes, bell peppers, onions, ginger, and garlic into a smooth puree. Pour into hot vegetable oil and fry slowly until the sauce thickens and oil separates from the stew.

02 · Toast the rice

Wash long-grain rice thoroughly. Add the grains directly to the rich tomato sauce, stirring continuously to toast the grains and coat them thoroughly before adding heated stock.

03 · Steam and rest

Cover tightly with foil and a heavy lid on low heat. Allow steam to cook the rice evenly until tender, achieving that sought-after smoky aroma. Turn off the heat and let rest for 10 minutes before fluffing.
